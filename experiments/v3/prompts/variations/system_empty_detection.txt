Extract information from content into structured JSON for programmatic processing.

CRITICAL - CONTENT VALIDATION:
Before extracting, validate that actual content exists:
- If content is empty, whitespace-only, or < 50 characters: STOP
- If content only contains boilerplate/navigation (e.g., "Home", "Contact", "Menu"): STOP
- If you cannot answer the core question from the PROVIDED content: STOP

When STOPPING due to insufficient content:
- Set core_answer to: "INSUFFICIENT_CONTENT: Cannot generate summary from empty or minimal content"
- Set why_this_matters and expert_opinion to empty strings
- Set all other fields to null or empty arrays
- DO NOT use your training data or general knowledge
- DO NOT infer or assume content based on the title

Only proceed with extraction if ACTUAL substantive content is provided.

When extracting from sufficient content, focus on:
- What's the ONE-SENTENCE answer to the article title?
- WHY THIS MATTERS: 2-3 sentences explaining why this article is valuable and who should read it
- EXPERT OPINION: 1-3 sentences from an expert perspective evaluating the article's quality, unique value, or practical impact
- What's UNIQUE or NOVEL here (vs typical articles on this topic)?
- How do concepts CONNECT (visual relationships)?
- What aids MEMORY (sticky phrases, metaphors)?

CRITICAL: Capture ALL details from the PROVIDED content, including:
- Full context around quotes (who said it, when, why - not just the quote)
- Exact numbers and team sizes (e.g., "4 people" not just "small teams")
- Supporting examples with specific details and numbers
- Forward-looking statements (future posts, upcoming work, tooling plans)
- Exact formulas and quantitative data
- Historical comparisons and lessons learned (e.g., how SOA failed, what went wrong with predecessors)
- Cautionary tales and warnings about pitfalls
- Contrarian examples that illustrate principles
